PRIVATE CULTURAL EXPERIENCE

Private Balinese Flute Workshop in Ubud

Step inside Bali's musical culture through bamboo craftsmanship, hands-on flute playing, a studio visit and a private live performance.

More than watching a performance

This private experience brings you closer to the sound and craft of the Balinese flute. The 45-minute workshop can be adapted to the guest, making it approachable for beginners while still offering a meaningful introduction to bamboo music.

Experience highlights

  • Meet and greet at the music studio.
  • Watch how a traditional Balinese bamboo flute is crafted.
  • Learn the basics of playing the Balinese flute.
  • Look around the studio and discover the creative environment.
  • Enjoy a live musical performance.
  • Private session, available by appointment.

Combine music with Ubud culture

The workshop can form the centerpiece of a relaxed private cultural outing around Ubud, paired with local heritage and rice-field scenery. The focus remains on a personal cultural encounter rather than a rushed sightseeing checklist.

Availability: workshops are by appointment and are not operated on a fixed daily schedule. Sunday is not offered for the tour program.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need musical experience?

No. The session can be adapted for beginners.

How long is the private workshop?

The core private workshop is approximately 45 minutes.

Is the workshop private?

Yes. It is arranged as a private session for your booking.
