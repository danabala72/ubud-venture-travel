EAST BALI • PRIVATE DAY TRIP

Lahangan Sweet, Amed Salt & Taman Ujung

Go beyond the classic East Bali route with Mount Agung viewpoints, a traditional coastal craft and one of Karangasem's most elegant royal water palaces.

East Bali beyond the usual photo stops

This private route is built around Lahangan Sweet rather than the crowded Lempuyang photo gate. Continue toward Amed to discover traditional salt-making when conditions allow, then explore the gardens and pools of Taman Ujung.

Highlights

  • Take in dramatic Mount Agung and East Bali views from Lahangan Sweet.
  • Discover traditional Amed salt-making when local production is available.
  • Walk through the pools, bridges and gardens of Taman Ujung Water Palace.
  • Travel privately with your own driver and flexible pacing.
  • Enjoy an East Bali route that combines scenery, local life and heritage.

Itinerary

Lahangan Sweet

Start with one of East Bali's striking mountain viewpoints. On a clear day, the elevated setting opens toward Mount Agung and the surrounding Karangasem landscape.

Amed traditional salt

Continue toward the coast to see a long-standing Amed tradition. Salt production depends on local and weather conditions, so the exact activity can vary on the day.

Taman Ujung Water Palace

Explore the former royal complex with its broad pools, bridges, gardens and mountain backdrop — a peaceful final contrast to the rugged viewpoint and coast.

Want to personalize the route? Selected original stops can be replaced with Lempuyang Temple, Tirta Gangga Water Palace or Virgin Beach. Custom-route entrance fees are paid directly by the guest.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lempuyang included in the main itinerary?

No. The main route is centered on Lahangan Sweet. Lempuyang can be selected as a replacement stop on a custom route.

Is salt-making always operating?

No. Traditional salt production can depend on weather and local working conditions.

Is the tour private?

Yes. Your booking has its own vehicle and driver.
