PRIVATE UBUD TOUR

Ubud Waterfalls, Sacred Monkey Forest & Rice Terraces

Experience three sides of Ubud in one private journey: tropical waterfalls, playful macaques in a sacred forest and Bali's iconic green rice terraces.

Discover Ubud at your own pace

This private Ubud day trip brings together nature, wildlife and classic Balinese scenery without the pace of a large group. Travel in a private air-conditioned vehicle with a local driver-guide and enjoy time at each stop.

Tour highlights

  • Walk through the lush Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary in Ubud.
  • See the layered green landscape of Tegallalang rice terraces.
  • Visit beautiful waterfalls surrounded by tropical vegetation.
  • Enjoy a private vehicle and flexible pacing throughout the day.
  • Travel with a friendly local driver-guide.

What to expect

Ubud waterfalls

Leave the busy center behind and head into Ubud's tropical landscape for a waterfall stop. Enjoy the scenery, take photos and cool down in the lush surroundings.

Sacred Monkey Forest

Explore one of Ubud's best-known natural and cultural landmarks, where Balinese temple architecture and forest paths are home to long-tailed macaques.

Tegallalang rice terraces

Finish with one of Bali's signature landscapes. See the terraced fields carved into the valley and learn how rice cultivation shapes rural life around Ubud.

Private means flexible. Timing can vary with traffic, weather and how long you choose to spend at each stop.

Who is this tour for?

A good choice for couples, families, friends and solo travelers who want to see Ubud's essential landscapes in a private setting rather than joining a large group.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a private tour?

Yes. The vehicle and driver-guide are reserved for your booking.

How many guests can join?

The standard private vehicle accommodates up to 6 guests.

Can the pace be adjusted?

Yes. This is a private experience, so the day has more flexibility than a shared group tour, subject to opening hours and travel time.
