Besakih Temple, Sidemen & Gembleng Private Day Trip

Discover Bali's spiritual heart at Besakih, slow down among Sidemen's rice terraces and traditional weaving, then cool off at hidden Gembleng Waterfall.

A quieter journey through East Bali

This private day trip combines one of Bali's most important temple complexes with the rural scenery and living traditions of Sidemen. Rather than rushing between photo stops, the route gives you a closer look at culture, craft and landscape.

Tour highlights

  • Visit iconic Besakih Temple, Bali's largest and most important temple complex.
  • Discover Sidemen's beautiful rice terraces and peaceful rural scenery.
  • Experience a short traditional weaving session in Sidemen.
  • Cool off at Gembleng Waterfall, a hidden gem surrounded by lush nature.
  • Enjoy a relaxed private day trip with your own driver and flexible pacing.

Itinerary

Besakih Temple

Explore the extensive temple complex on the slopes of Mount Agung and learn about its place in Balinese Hindu life.

Sidemen rice terraces & weaving

Continue into Sidemen's countryside for rice-field views and a closer look at traditional textile craft. The weaving experience is designed as a short, approachable introduction rather than a long formal class.

Gembleng Waterfall

End among tropical greenery at Gembleng, known for its natural pools and peaceful hillside setting.

Frequently asked questions

Is the trip private?

Yes. Your group travels in its own vehicle with a driver-guide.

Is lunch included?

No. A lunch stop can be made during the day and you pay directly for what you order.

How long is the weaving experience?

The mini weaving experience is typically around 30–45 minutes.
